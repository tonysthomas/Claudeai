# Product lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **A real gap, said simply.** "People already use AI at work but were never trained" is a one-sentence problem. The plan's figures (75% use it, 39% trained) are the founder's own citations and have not been checked.
- **Governance as the wedge.** This is the most specific idea in the plan. A governance starter kit or workshop for an SME owner in the UAE is a product you can picture someone buying.
- **Discipline in the details.** The fixed post format, the weekly rhythm and the rule never to make up a statistic are care in the parts nobody checks, and the Product lens rewards that.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No focus.** Three pillars, five levels, five certifications, six revenue lines and three platforms, all launched by one person in six months. Nothing gets done brilliantly. The pitch needs a paragraph, so by this lens the product is not clear yet.
2. **The buyer journey is full of handoffs.** A faceless Instagram account sends people to a LinkedIn account with the founder's face, then to an email list, an ebook, a workshop and a certificate. The reach audience (frontline staff, students) is not the paying audience (SME owners, HR, executives). Every handoff loses people, and nobody has designed the path from the person who saves a post to the person who signs a $5,000 workshop.
3. **The flagship is not defined.** Workshop pricing runs from $500 to $5,000+ per seat. Consulting, course and cohort have no price yet. Certifications with no stated accreditation behind them risk feeling hollow to a compliance buyer, who is exactly the person the governance wedge targets.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence: what does an SME owner buy from you, and what can they do afterwards that they could not do before?
- Walk me through the first five minutes of your best paid product. What does the buyer see, hold and leave with?
- Which four of the six revenue lines would you cut this year?
- What did Phase 1 actually deliver against its gate (posts, subscribers, save rate)? What has Phase 2 delivered so far?
- How does a follower on the faceless Instagram account end up paying, and who in that path holds the budget?
- Who else sells governance training to UAE SMEs, and what do their buyers complain about? (Open question for /founder-competitors.)
- Will an HR or compliance lead pay for this, and at what price? (Open question for /founder-consumer and /founder-pricing.)
- What does it cost to win one corporate client, and how many hours a week can you put in? (Open question for /founder-cfo.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Cut to one flagship product: a governance workshop and starter kit for UAE SME leaders, with one fixed price. Park the five-level academy, the certifications and the course until the flagship sells again and again.
2. Design that buyer's whole experience from the first touch to the follow-up, and make the channel they already use (probably LinkedIn) the main path, not the faceless reach account.
3. Show real Phase 1 and Phase 2 numbers, plus at least 3 paid governance engagements, before building anything at Levels 1–2 or 5.
