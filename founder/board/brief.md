# It's AI Education

- What it is: A plain-English AI education brand and academy that takes non-technical professionals and SMEs "From AI Awareness to AI Leadership" through a five-level ladder (Fluency → Application → Adoption → Governance → Leadership), with responsible-AI governance as the differentiating wedge.
- Who it is for: Masses (for reach): non-technical knowledge workers, frontline and retail staff, managers and students who already use AI but were never trained. Revenue: SME owners, HR, L&D and department heads, compliance, and executives, starting in Dubai / UAE and the wider GCC.
- What it sells, at what price:
  - Free: Instagram (faceless), YouTube, LinkedIn (face-led) content and lead magnets (EU AI Act 1-pager, AI tool audit checklist).
  - Digital products: ebook / AI Fluency Toolkit / Prompt Library / AI Governance Starter Kit, $19–49 (first ebook planned at $19–27).
  - 1-1 time and SME consulting (Phase 3, price not yet set).
  - Corporate workshops (Fluency / Governance / Adoption), $500–5,000+ per seat.
  - E-learning course + first cohort (Phase 4, price not yet set).
  - Academy certifications (AI Fluency Foundation → Certified AI Transformation Leader), B2B licensing under $20k.
- Where and how: Online, run from Dubai. Instagram builds reach (faceless), LinkedIn carries the founder's face and closes SME/corporate deals, YouTube builds depth, the email list is the owned asset. Delivery: digital downloads, live workshops, 1-1 consulting, e-learning course and cohorts, B2B licences.
- Budget and constraints: Budget not stated. Solo founder (Tony Thomas), retail training / L&D background, IAPP AIGP status cited in the plan. Brand rules: faceless Instagram, one small problem per post, never fabricate a statistic, zero selling in Phase 1. Timeline: Phase 1 Authority 18 Jul–31 Aug 2026 · Phase 2 First Dollar Sep (ebook, 25+ sales, 400+ subs) · Phase 3 High-Ticket Oct (3–5 paid engagements, 700+ subs) · Phase 4 Scale Nov–Dec (course + cohort, 1,500+ subs, 3 revenue lines).

## More context from the founder's plan

**Purpose.** Close the gap between AI adoption and AI understanding. The plan cites: 75% of knowledge workers use AI at work; only 39% have had any training. Teach the most-exposed people properly, in plain English, one small lesson at a time.

**Vision.** Become the trusted plain-English authority on AI fluency and governance for non-technical professionals and SMEs: start faceless on Instagram, grow into a face-led education platform from Dubai.

**Strategy (founder's words).** "Masses-first for reach, SME-adjacent for revenue. Instagram stays faceless and builds reach; LinkedIn carries the face and closes the money; the email list is the asset that outlives both. Governance is the wedge." The plan claims governance is the weakest pillar across competitors (no competitor analysis has been run yet).

**Three pillars.** AI Fluency (fundamentals; beginners, professionals, managers, students) · AI Governance (policies, data privacy, risk, human oversight, compliance; business leaders, HR, L&D, executives) · AI Adoption (change management, AI champions, measuring adoption; organisations, L&D, HR, managers).

**Five-level academy, each with a certification.**
1. AI Fluency → "AI Fluency Foundation" (students, frontline, beginners)
2. AI Application → "AI Practitioner" (knowledge workers, managers, retail, entrepreneurs): prompting, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, productivity
3. AI Adoption → "AI Adoption Specialist" (managers, HR, L&D, department heads)
4. AI Governance → "AI Governance Professional" (executives, compliance, HR, L&D)
5. AI Leadership → "Certified AI Transformation Leader" (directors, CEOs, entrepreneurs)

**Channel → level map.** Instagram → Level 1 · YouTube → Levels 1–2 · Workshops → Level 3 · Corporate programmes → Levels 3–4 · Executive advisory → Level 5.

**Content system.** 1–2 Instagram posts a day (65 posts planned in Phase 1), format: Situation → Action → Proof → Name the concept → Transfer. Weekly rhythm: write Sunday, design Monday, record Tuesday, publish and engage Wed–Sat, log Friday. DM automation via ManyChat delivers lead magnets from comment keywords. LinkedIn 2–5 posts/week; YouTube 2–3 Shorts + 1 long-form/week.

**Phase 1 gate (by 31 Aug 2026).** 40+ posts · 150+ email subscribers · LinkedIn live with 12+ posts · 3+ posts with outsized reach · save rate trending up over the last 10 posts · ebook outline drafted · DM automation reliable. Actual results against this gate have not been provided.

**Market signals the plan cites.** UAE AI adoption 70.1% (#1 globally) · ~82% completion for instructor-led vs 19% self-paced · EU AI Act full application Aug 2026 · AI-in-education market $6.9–18.9B. Not independently verified here.

**Unknowns (do not invent them).** Startup budget and monthly spend; founder's weekly hours (whether this runs alongside a job); current follower, subscriber and revenue numbers; prices for consulting, course and cohort; cost to acquire a corporate client; named competitors.
