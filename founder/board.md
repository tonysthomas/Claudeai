## Risks all three raised (most dangerous first)

1. **Too broad, no focus.** 3 pillars × 5 levels × 5 certifications × 6 revenue lines × 3 platforms, solo, in six months. All three lenses call this the top or second risk.
2. **No proof yet.** No Phase 1/2 results, no paid clients, no case studies and no testimonials. The certifications are self-issued, and the IAPP AIGP is the only outside credential.
3. **The governance gap is unverified.** "Governance is the weakest pillar across competitors" has not been tested, because no competitor map exists yet.
4. **The people who follow aren't the people who pay.** Frontline workers and students follow the faceless Instagram account. The money comes from SME owners, HR and executives on LinkedIn, and no designed path connects the two.
5. **The certifications have no outside backing.** Nothing says an employer or regulator recognises them, which matters most to compliance buyers (Offers and Monopoly lenses raised the self-issued certifications; Product and Monopoly asked about accreditation).

## Where they disagree

- **Monopoly votes PASS. The other two vote FUND IF.** The disagreement is about whether anything can be defended against copycats. Offers and Product think a well-packaged, proven governance offer is enough to start. Monopoly says plain-English content, prompt libraries and checklists can be copied within weeks, so it needs a durable asset first: a proprietary method, a recognised credential or partnership, or a sector-specific asset.
- **Offers is the only lens that asks for a guarantee and a stacked, named offer.** Product cares more about the buyer's end-to-end experience than about the deal terms.

## Conditions (merged checklist for the rest of the pack)

- [ ] **Cut to one flagship.** A named AI-governance offer for UAE/GCC SME leaders: written AI policy, risk register and a staff training session, at **one fixed price**. Park Levels 1–2 and 5, the certifications, the course and the cohort. *(all three)*
- [ ] **Add a guarantee and a delivery date**, e.g. "signed AI policy in 30 days or the next session is free". *(Offers → /founder-offer)*
- [ ] **3+ paid (or deeply discounted) governance engagements** with documented before/after results. *(all three)*
- [ ] **Report the real Phase 1 gate and Phase 2 numbers**: posts, subscribers, save rate, ebook sales. *(all three)*
- [ ] **Prove the wedge.** Map who already sells AI governance training to UAE SMEs, at what price, and what their buyers complain about. *(→ /founder-competitors)*
- [ ] **Confirm GCC buyers feel the pain.** The EU AI Act binds only firms that serve the EU, so identify which UAE/GCC rules or buyer pressures apply. *(→ /founder-consumer, /founder-competitors)*
- [ ] **Know the economics.** Find the workshop margin after delivery, the cost to win one corporate client, payback, and the founder's weekly hours. *(→ /founder-cfo)*
- [ ] **Price the stack, not the ebook.** Treat the $19–49 products as lead magnets. *(Offers → /founder-pricing)*
- [ ] **Design the buyer's journey.** Make LinkedIn and email the main paid path, and treat Instagram as reach only. *(Product → /founder-marketing)*
- [ ] **Name a path to durability**: a proprietary method or data, a recognised credential or partner, or a sector-specific asset. *(Monopoly)*

## The strongest version the board can see

It is narrower than what was pitched. The academy, the five-level ladder and the mass-audience certificates come later, if at all. What the board would fund now is **an AI governance and adoption sprint for UAE and GCC SMEs**: one named, fixed-price package (AI usage policy, risk register, a staff session and a 30-day check), sold face-to-face on LinkedIn and email, guaranteed, and proven with three paid pilots before anything else gets built. The founder's retail L&D background and IAPP AIGP point to a sharper first niche: **GCC retail and frontline-heavy businesses**. In those businesses, large numbers of untrained staff already use AI with customer data, and few competitors speak that language. That niche is the board's suggestion, not part of the plan, and /founder-competitors and /founder-consumer should test it. Instagram keeps building reach, but it is a feeder, not the business. The certifications become a moat only if an outside body recognises them.
